V Vyora Product Vision

India's AI-first, offline-first Business OS for MSMEs

Not another billing app. One platform that becomes whatever business it's given — a pharmacy, a restaurant, a jeweller — with billing, inventory, GST, marketing, and an AI copilot that works with zero internet and total privacy.

An owner says “I run a pharmacy” — and the app instantly adapts with pharmacy fields, expiry tracking, GST rules, reports, and AI suggestions. Another says “I run a restaurant” — and the same platform becomes a restaurant POS with tables, KOT, and delivery. The core never changes; only the configuration does.
