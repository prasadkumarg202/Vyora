V Vyora Navigation & Information Architecture

How the whole product is organised

Eighteen modules grouped into seven navigation zones. Menus adapt to the business type and the user's role; global tools (search, Copilot, sync) stay constant. This map is the blueprint every screen slots into.

A

Navigation model

A persistent left sidebar groups modules by intent — Overview, Sell, Buy, Catalog, Contacts, Finance, Workspace. A fixed top bar carries the business switcher, global search, the Copilot, and sync status.
