V Vyora Authentication · Doc 07

Phone-first login that unlocks an encryption key

MSME owners log in with a phone number and OTP — no passwords to forget. Behind it, authentication also unwraps the company's data-encryption key, binds the session to a device, and issues short-lived tokens carrying the tenant claim.

Tokens & the JWT claim set

Access token15 min

Short-lived JWT sent as a Bearer header. Carries tenant + role; RLS reads it directly.

Refresh token30 days · rotating

Stored in secure storage, bound to device_id. Rotated on every use; reuse = breach → revoke all.
