V Vyora Sync Engine · Doc 09

A durable outbox that drains without losing a byte

The sync engine turns local writes into a queue of encrypted envelopes, flushes them to the server on a delta cursor, resolves conflicts deterministically, and fans changes back to every device via Realtime. This doc is the algorithm; Offline Architecture has the interactive demo.
